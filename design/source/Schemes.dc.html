Mudra loan (Shishu)முத்ரா கடன்
May be eligible
Small business loans from banks, up to ₹50,000 in the Shishu category, for tools or working capital.
  • Bring Aadhaar, bank passbook and your Kaithozhil earnings statement
  • Apply at your bank branch
PM Vishwakarmaபிஎம் விஸ்வகர்மா
Not for your trade
For 18 traditional trades such as carpenter, mason, tailor and barber: toolkit support, training and low-interest loans.
  • Electricians are not one of the listed trades
  • Tell a carpenter, mason or tailor friend about it
